# Prompts de videos contextuales

**Fecha:** 2026-09-07  
**Proyecto:** Urticaria Novartis

## Video base, sin comezón

```text
Create a subtle cinematic 8-second seamless loop from this reference image.

Preserve the exact composition:
- inverted coupe glass on the right side
- miniature seated woman inside the glass
- wide negative space on the left for website copy
- clear lower-right table area for a numeric counter
- no text, no logos, no overlays, no extra people

Animate only the environment:
soft natural daylight slowly changes across the table,
delicate plant leaves move slightly in the background,
subtle realistic reflections shimmer on the glass,
the woman remains completely still,
the camera is locked off with no zoom or pan.

Style:
premium editorial healthcare campaign,
photorealistic,
quiet, elegant, emotionally calm,
seamless loop,
no abrupt transitions.
```

## Variante con comezón

```text
Create a subtle cinematic 8-second seamless loop from this reference video.

Preserve the exact original composition and identity:
- same inverted coupe glass in the same position
- same miniature woman seated inside the glass
- same camera angle, framing, lighting and environment
- same wide negative space for website copy
- no text, no logos, no extra people
- do not change the woman’s face, clothing, body proportions or position inside the glass

Animate only one subtle action:
the woman slowly and gently raises one hand to scratch her forearm once, then calmly returns to her original seated pose.
The movement should feel natural, restrained and emotionally realistic.
Do not exaggerate the scratching and do not add visible wounds, redness or medical graphics.

The glass, table, reflections and background remain stable.
Add only delicate realistic reflections moving across the glass.
Camera locked off, no zoom, no pan, no reframing.

Style:
premium editorial healthcare campaign,
photorealistic,
quiet and emotionally sensitive,
subtle human gesture,
seamless loop,
no abrupt transitions.
```
